# 90-day content and execution roadmap

**Rules for every piece:** one reason to exist; facts about D’Vorsche products must be verified by the brand owner first; no fabricated first-hand experience or testing; no invented origin, material, certification or sustainability claims; link to the relevant collection or product with descriptive anchors; unique images with accurate alt text. Do not publish more than the team can fact-check.

Everything here is **planned, not written**. The article pages are not built because their content depends on product facts (materials, card capacity, dimensions) that are not yet confirmed.

## Execution plan
**Days 0-30 (foundation)**
- Owner: set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` on the production host, verify the domain in Search Console, submit `https://{domain}/sitemap.xml`, replace placeholder products with real data and photography, add real product images to the Product schema.
- Run the Rich Results Test on one product URL and Lighthouse/PageSpeed Insights on home, collection and product pages (baseline).
- Trademark counsel review of the name.
- Write and fact-check articles 1 and 2. Build the press page with real facts.
- Add Merchant Center free listings once product images, GTINs/SKUs, shipping and returns data exist.

**Days 31-60 (content and authority)**
- Publish articles 1-3 and the gift guide (4) ahead of the next gifting season.
- Pitch two to four roundup editors (FashionBeans-type, Gear Patrol-type) with samples on request.
- Review Search Console: indexed pages, impressions by query and country, branded vs non-branded, "dvorsche" misspelling and Porsche-confusion queries.
- Decide on UK storefront readiness (GBP, shipping, returns).

**Days 61-90 (scale only what works)**
- Expand articles that earn impressions; update or retire those that do not.
- If UK is ready, launch `/en-gb/` with reciprocal hreflang. Otherwise postpone.
- Evaluate Phase 2 markets with real data; decide on translation investment.
- Revisit the keyword CSV with a proper keyword tool for volumes and difficulty.

## KPIs (set targets only after a baseline)
Indexed eligible pages; non-branded impressions and clicks; branded impressions (watch Porsche confusion); impressions for the target collection and guide queries; organic product-page visits; add-to-cart rate from organic sessions; organic conversion rate; revenue attributable to organic search where measurement is reliable (GA4 consent limits this in the UK/EU/Canada).
